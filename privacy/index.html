Privacy Policy

The short version

We use your resume only to find jobs for you. We never sell your data or share it with employers. Delete everything any time with /deleteme.

What we collect

  • Telegram account: your ID, name and username.
  • Resume: the PDF you upload, and the skills and experience we find in it.
  • Preferences: roles, cities, companies and anything you've asked us to hide.
  • Activity: the jobs we've sent you, what you marked as applied or skipped, and which link brought you to Watcher.
  • Payments: Telegram's payment ID, the amount and the date. We never see your card or bank details.

How we use it

To match and send you jobs, run your subscription, answer support requests, and keep the service secure. Nothing else.

Your resume and AI

Our own software reads your resume and does the matching. Your resume is never sent to an AI service that trains on the data it receives.

Sharing

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with employers. The cloud and messaging providers that run Watcher handle it only on our behalf.

Security

Resumes and bot credentials are stored encrypted, and access to them is restricted.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you use Watcher. A new resume replaces the old one. /deleteme permanently deletes your resume, profile, preferences and history. We keep payment records only as long as the law requires for accounting.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data, email watcher101.hq@gmail.com.
