Quick start

  1. Open @WatcherJobsBot, tap Start, then Create my job bot.
  2. Open your new bot and send your resume as a PDF file (📎 → File).
  3. Check your skills, experience, roles, cities and companies. That's it: matches arrive in your bot.

Your job bot

/settingsChange skills, experience, roles, cities, companies, hidden words
/resumeGet your resume back, or send a new one
/pause · /unpauseStop or restart alerts
/appliedJobs you marked as applied
/planYour plan and today's matches
/inviteLet up to 10 people use your bot
/deletemeDelete all your data

On every alert

Apply ↗Opens the job on the company's site
✅ AppliedSaves it to /applied
🙈 SkipHides it
💡 Why?Shows which of your skills matched

The HQ bot

/plan · /cancelUpgrade to Pro, or stop renewal
/supportGet help
/paysupportPayment problems

Tip

Getting a few jobs you don't want? Add a word to hide them, e.g. PHP or night shift, in /settings → Hide.
