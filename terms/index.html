Terms of Service

The short version

Watcher sends new jobs that match your resume to your own Telegram bot. Pro is a monthly subscription paid in Telegram Stars. Cancel any time, and Pro stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.

The service

Watcher sends you job postings that match your resume and preferences. Employers can change or close a posting at any time, so check the details on their site before you apply. We can't promise a set number of matches, interviews or offers. Watcher isn't affiliated with the companies it lists.

You can add the companies you want to follow. Watcher supports most companies, but not every one. If a company can't be followed, we'll tell you when you add it.

Your bot

Your personal bot is created in your Telegram account and run by Watcher. If you delete it, alerts stop; you can create a new one from the HQ bot at any time.

Fair use

Use Watcher for your own job search. Don't upload someone else's resume, resell matches, or try to get around plan limits.

Pro subscription

Pro is billed every 30 days in Telegram Stars through the HQ bot. You'll see the price before you pay. It renews automatically until you cancel with /cancel in the HQ bot. When you cancel, Pro stays active until the end of the current period and then doesn't renew.

Payments and refunds

Payments are non-refundable, including for partly used months. If something goes wrong with a payment (for example, you were charged but Pro didn't switch on), send /paysupport to the HQ bot and we'll put it right. Watcher handles all purchases made in its bots, so Telegram support can't help with them.

Changes

We may improve the service or update these terms. Before a significant change takes effect, we'll tell you in the bot.
