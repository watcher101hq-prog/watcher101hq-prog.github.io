Commands

Type / in the chat or tap ☰ Menu to see them. Most things also work with buttons.

Your job bot

/settingsEverything in one place: skills, experience, roles, cities, companies, company type, work mode, hidden words, ideal job, quiet nights and tips. Tap a line to change it.
/companiesThe companies you follow. Tap one to unfollow it.
/addcompanyFollow a new company. Type a name or paste its careers link, e.g. /addcompany Stripe.
/resumeGet your resume back, or send a new one
/appliedJobs you marked as applied
/pause · /unpauseStop or restart alerts
/planYour plan and today's matches
/shareSend Watcher to a friend, so they can make their own bot
/helpThe quick guide
/supportAsk us anything
/deletemePermanently delete your resume, profile, preferences and history

On every job

Apply ↗Opens the job on the company's site
✅ AppliedSaves it to /applied
🙈 SkipHides it. Tap Undo to bring it back.
💡 Why?Shows which of your skills matched, and what's missing

The HQ bot

/mybotOpen your job bot, or make a new one if you deleted it
/planUpgrade to Pro, or see when it renews
/cancelStop Pro from renewing. It stays on until the end of the month you've paid for.
/paysupportA problem with a payment
/supportAsk us anything
/terms · /privacyOur terms and privacy policy

Shortcuts

  • Send a PDF at any time to replace your resume.
  • In the middle of a step, any command cancels it.
